About us

Welcome to Turon-Da-Go

At Turon-Da-Go, we believe that school breaks should be sweet, comforting, and hassle-free. Born out of a passion for innovation and student enterprise at La Salle College Antipolo, our team set out to transform a beloved Filipino staple into an unforgettable dessert experience.

The Story Behind Saba Zero

We noticed a common dilemma during quick study breaks: students were constantly torn between hot, savory finger foods and cold, refreshing treats. That is where our flagship product, Saba Zero, was born. By pairing freshly fried, warm mini-turons with creamy ice cream and specialty dessert drizzles, we created the ultimate hot-and-cold fusion snack.

Our Lasallian Spirit

Grounded in our core values of Best People, Honesty, Innovation, and Efficiency, we operate directly within campus at Br. Donato Hall. We are committed to using quality local ingredients, maintaining strict food safety, and delivering high-value desserts that make every student's merienda special.

Our mission

Turon-Da-Go is dedicated to serving every La Salle College Antipolo student with fresh, high-quality, and innovative Saba-Zero desserts made from the finest ingredients, with strict food safety and hygiene and great value for money, guided by Lasallian values of excellence and service.

Our vision

To become a recognized, distinctive, trend-setting student enterprise brand within La Salle College Antipolo and beyond, continuously adding flavors and turning a classic Filipino merienda favorite into a modern, memorable dessert experience.
